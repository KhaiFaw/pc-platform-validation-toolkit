# Security and privacy

Please do not include machine identifiers, credentials, private paths, or raw environment dumps in issue reports or committed fixtures.

The toolkit is designed to operate without administrator privileges. It must not read privileged registers, change firmware or power settings, access raw disks, or execute arbitrary commands from a test plan.

Security reports should identify the affected version, expected boundary, observed behaviour, and a minimal sanitized reproduction. Until a private reporting channel is established, do not publish exploit details or sensitive evidence in a public issue.
